Behind EXPRESO

From components to complete vehicles.

RIT Enterprise imports high-grade components and assembles complete electric three-wheelers from the ground up under our own EXPRESO brand.

What you see here is the real operation: from bulk imported parts stored in our warehouse to hands-on assembly by our skilled technicians, ending with road-ready vehicles on the showroom floor. Every step happens under our roof, giving you a closer look at the quality, craftsmanship, and people behind every EXPRESO.
